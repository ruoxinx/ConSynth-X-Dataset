00 — Motivation

Why ConSynth-X?

Computer vision models for construction site monitoring have demonstrated strong performance under controlled conditions — but construction sites don't stop for good weather. They operate continuously through rain, snow, fog, darkness, and glare, exposing deployed vision systems to conditions rarely represented in training data.

The gap is measurable: our cross-condition evaluation shows object detection accuracy drops 40–50% when models trained on clear-weather images are tested under adverse conditions. Existing construction datasets (Construction Site 10K, SODA) predominantly contain favorable-condition imagery, and prior synthetic augmentation efforts remain limited in scale (506 images in ExtCon), method diversity (style transfer only), and condition coverage (no snow, nighttime, or scale variation).

ConSynth-X addresses this gap with three complementary synthetic augmentation pipelines — neural style transfer, text-guided diffusion, and outpainting — applied to two large-scale source datasets, producing 125,000+ images spanning weather, lighting, and scale conditions. Each image carries multi-label condition annotations validated through five independent quality metrics. The result is the first large-scale, publicly available dataset that systematically pairs construction site imagery with controlled extreme conditions and comprehensive multi-metric validation.

07 — Key Findings

What the Data Reveals

Empirical observations from our multi-metric validation across augmentation methods.

Two Methods Are Complementary

Style transfer creates visually obvious weather (70-95% classifier accuracy) while IP2P diffusion preserves texture fidelity 5x better. Neither alone is sufficient.

Style Transfer: High recognizability, more artifacts
IP2P Diffusion: Natural texture, subtle effects

Night = Strongest Semantic Shift

CycleGAN-Turbo night translation achieves -34% FID improvement and 38% Mahalanobis gap closure in CLIP feature space.

FID: 178.9 vs 270.5 (baseline) — strongest of all conditions

IP2P Snow Contradiction

IP2P snow achieves highest texture fidelity (0.862 belief score) but only 25.7% weather classifier accuracy. Under active investigation.

Hypothesis: Creates atmospheric effects (gray skies) without visible snow particles

VLM Weakness Confirmed

Best VLM (InternVL2.5-26B) achieves only F1=0.124 on clean construction images, with 20-24% additional drops under extreme conditions.

LLaVA-7B: 100% failure rate — validates need for Papers 2 & 3
