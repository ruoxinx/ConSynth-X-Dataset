Tutorial

Read through these guidelines before starting the evaluation tasks.

1

About This Study

We are evaluating the visual quality of synthetic construction site images. These images were generated by applying weather and lighting augmentations (rain, snow, night, etc.) to real construction photographs. Your task is to judge how realistic these augmentations look.

2

Task 1: Real vs Synthetic (Turing Test)

You will see one image at a time. Some are real photographs of weather conditions (from driving datasets), and some are synthetically augmented construction images. Decide: is this image real or synthetic?

Use keyboard shortcuts: R for Real, S for Synthetic.

3

Task 2: Realism Rating (MOS)

You will see synthetic images only. Rate how realistic the weather or lighting condition looks on a scale of 1 to 5:

  • 1 — Completely fake, obvious artifacts
  • 2 — Mostly fake, noticeable issues
  • 3 — Neutral, some parts look real, some don't
  • 4 — Mostly realistic, minor imperfections
  • 5 — Completely realistic, indistinguishable from real

Use keyboard shortcuts: 1 through 5.

4

Task 3: Condition Recognition

You will see a synthetic image without any label. Identify what weather or lighting condition you see. Choose from: Clear, Rain, Snow, Night, Night+Rain, Night+Snow, Fog, or Small/Far objects.

Use keyboard shortcuts: 1 through 8.

5

Tips

Trust your first impression — don't overthink it. Look at the overall atmosphere, lighting consistency, and whether weather effects (rain drops, snow flakes, darkness) blend naturally with the scene. There are no wrong answers; we are measuring human perception.
